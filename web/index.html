How the simulation works and what it cannot know
What Nemotron does

Reads a plain-language household preference and returns a strict set of constraints: battery reserve, local-first choice, evening-sharing choice and a no-discharge window. The server validates every field.

What the simulator does

Applies those constraints to three homes, hour by hour. It calculates solar use, battery flows, local allocation, grid import/export and energy cost. The model never supplies a number.

What it cannot know

Real meter data, network limits, retailer terms, settlement, taxes, losses, battery degradation and whether a neighbour consents. This is a prototype, not a market or grid study.
